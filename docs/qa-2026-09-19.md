# Product and educational QA — 19 September 2026

This review found product, security, and teaching defects. Code fixes are included in this release. Following the owner’s explicit authorization, the database corrections were applied to production and verified on 19 September 2026. This is an engineering and source-based linguistic review, not independent teacher approval, a penetration-test certification, or a guarantee that every future AI response is correct.

## Scope

Reviewed the adopted A1–C2 curriculum plan and generated 87-module catalog, the 11 versioned rule lessons and seven-step past-narration practice path, 18 sample lessons (72 questions), the eight-question grammar check, all 150 authored vocabulary entries with examples/notes, and 14 activities across six guided lessons reconstructed from the migrations. Reviewed authentication, admin authorization, owner-scoped storage, learning evidence, API inputs, AI grading/generation instructions, photo handling, transcription, speech, dependency advisories, and production session configuration. Existing learner-authored lists and arbitrary past/future AI outputs are outside exhaustive linguistic validation.

The full curriculum is a coverage plan. Most modules remain outlines; the implemented lesson count must not be described as a complete A1–C2 course. Difficulty depends on the task and expected control, not simply the presence of one form or uncommon word. C1/C2 sample labels are instructional scope, not independently calibrated item levels.

## Source checks

Consulted 19 September 2026; examples and explanations in this release are original.

- [Instituto Cervantes A1–A2 grammar](https://cvc.cervantes.es/ensenanza/biblioteca_ele/plan_curricular/niveles/02_gramatica_inventario_a1-a2.htm): checked adjective agreement and the present/imperfect/preterite section references used by the implemented lessons.
- [Instituto Cervantes B1–B2 grammar](https://cvc.cervantes.es/ensenanza/biblioteca_ele/plan_curricular/niveles/02_gramatica_inventario_b1-b2.htm): checked past relations and the pluperfect reference. Earlier-level forms can legitimately be integrated in B2 tasks.
- [Instituto Cervantes C1–C2 functions](https://cvc.cervantes.es/ensenanza/biblioteca_ele/plan_curricular/niveles/05_funciones_inventario_c1-c2.htm): advanced interaction provides a basis for refining stance and register; this is a design rationale, not certification of individual item levels.
- [RAE–ASALE, *el*, §§2.1–2.3](https://www.rae.es/dpd/el): stressed-a feminine nouns retain feminine agreement; exceptions exist, including letter names and certain person-denoting nouns.
- [RAE–ASALE, indirect objects](https://www.rae.es/gtg/complemento-indirecto): tonic personal object pronouns require corresponding clitics in the construction under review.
- [RAE–ASALE, *si*, §1.1](https://www.rae.es/dpd/si): ordinary open conditions differ from hypothetical conditions and indirect questions.
- [Council of Europe, relating examinations to CEFR](https://www.coe.int/en/web/common-european-framework-reference-languages/relating-examinations-to-the-cefr) and [self-assessment grid](https://www.coe.int/en/web/portfolio/self-assessment-grid): CEFR claims concern broader communicative activity and require an assessment rationale; a short grammar score cannot establish general proficiency.

## Remaining release limitations

1. Independent qualified Spanish-teacher review and learner calibration are still required for the implemented lesson corpus, distractors, level placement, and AI grading. Teacher review status stays pending. More sample questions do not resolve this requirement.
2. Full atomic-rule/source mapping, comprehensive A1–C2 implementation, independently graded writing/speaking, and validated retention thresholds remain unfinished. The UI distinguishes outlines, previews, practice, and provisional AI evidence.
3. Supabase was verified on Free, Time-box user sessions `0 / never`, and access-token expiry `3600` seconds. Upgrade to Pro or above and set the session timebox to `168` hours. No plan purchase or authentication-policy change was performed.
4. The closed-alpha approval UI remains a development preview, as documented in `alpha-approval.md`; it does not enforce invitations. Preserve the current private Sites audience. Do not claim public launch readiness.
5. CSP still allows inline scripts for the current framework. React escapes learner text and no learner-controlled HTML execution was found in the reviewed application paths; a nonce-based CSP and dedicated penetration test remain defense-in-depth work.
6. AI schemas and prompts constrain behavior but do not ensure factual or linguistic truth. Generated vocabulary and feedback remain provisional, labeled, and challengeable. Provider retention is not guaranteed to be zero by `store: false`.
